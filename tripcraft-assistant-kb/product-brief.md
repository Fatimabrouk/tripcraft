# Product Brief — tripcraft (rough draft)

_Internal working doc. Not final copy. Pulled together from the PM notes, design review, and the beta wrap-up call. Use this as the source of truth for the launch._

## The one-liner
tripcraft is an AI trip planner built into the tripcraft app. Describe the trip you want in plain language and tripcraft builds a day-by-day itinerary you can book — stays, activities, and food — in a few taps.

## The problem we're solving
People love the *idea* of planning a trip and hate the *reality* of it. Our research and support tickets keep surfacing the same thing:
- Travelers open 15–20 browser tabs and give up halfway.
- They spend hours stitching together stays, things to do, and restaurants across different sites.
- First-time visitors to a city don't know what's actually worth their time.
- Group and family trips make all of this 3x harder.

The average tripcraft user who eventually books a trip spends **4+ hours** researching across the week before they book. Most of that happens *outside* our app.

## How it works (v1, launch scope)
1. The traveler types a request in natural language, e.g. _"5 days in Lisbon in October, mid-budget, we love food and coffee, not too much walking."_
2. tripcraft  generates a full day-by-day itinerary: where to stay, 2–3 activities per day, and food suggestions, all matched to the budget and vibe.
3. Everything in the itinerary is **bookable inside tripcraft** — stays and activities link straight to our existing inventory.
4. The traveler can tweak by chatting back ("swap day 3 for something more relaxed", "cheaper hotel"), and the  updates.
5. When they're happy, they book the whole trip in a few taps.

## Who it's for
- **Primary:** time-poor leisure travelers, 25–65, who already book stays with us but  the rest elsewhere.
- **Secondary:** first-time visitors to a city who want a "someone who knows the place" shortcut.
- **Also lands well with:** couples and small groups planning together.

## What's new / why it matters
- It's the first time the whole trip — stay + activities + food — comes together in **one place, in minutes instead of hours**.
- It turns tripcraft from "a place you book a hotel" into "a place you  the whole trip."
- Strategically: activities and experiences are higher-margin than stays, and  drives travelers toward booking them with us instead of on arrival.

## Beta results (6-week early access, ~4,200 travelers)
- **71%** of people who generated a plan booked at least one thing from it.
- Median time from "open app" to "trip booked" dropped from ~4 hours (self-reported prior trips) to **under 25 minutes**.
- **+32%** attach rate on activities vs. users who booked a stay without Plan.
- NPS for the beta feature: **62**.
- Most-loved: the "just chat to change it" editing. Most-requested: multi-city trips (not in v1).

## Pricing / availability
- tripcraft  is **free for all tripcraft users** at launch. No new paywall.
- It's part of the strategy to grow bookings, not a standalone paid product.
- (A premium "tripcraft+" tier may bundle extras later — out of scope for this launch.)

## Launch
- in English first; more languages to follow.
- Available on web.

## Known limits / say-this-carefully
- **v1 is single-city only.** Multi-city and multi-country trips are coming but not at launch — don't over-promise.
- Itineraries are suggestions, not guarantees — availability and prices are confirmed at booking.
- It plans leisure trips well; it's not tuned for business travel yet.
